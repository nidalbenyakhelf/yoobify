    How it works

    Three simple steps to discover music across borders.

    1. Paste Your Playlists

    Drop links from Spotify or Deezer. You can paste multiple playlists to compare them.

    2. We Find the Overlap

    Our algorithm identifies songs that appear in multiple playlists and analyzes their shared traits.

    3. Discover New Music

    Based on common tracks, we suggest similar songs from other languages and cultures you might love.

    Our algorithm (in simple words)

    We don't use magic. We use math and music theory.

    • Track Matching: We compare song titles, artists, and duration to find exact matches across platforms.
    • Audio Features: We analyze tempo (BPM), key, energy, and mood to find similar songs.
    • Cultural Bridges: If you like Arabic pop with 120 BPM, we'll find Turkish, Spanish, or French songs with similar vibes.

    Want to improve our algorithm? Check the contribute section below.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is yoobify free?

    Yes, completely free and open source. No ads, no premium plans, no hidden costs.

    Do I need to create an account?

    No. Just paste your playlist links and go. We don't store your personal data.

    Which platforms are supported right now?

    Currently only Spotify and Deezer. We hope to add Apple Music, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, and Anghami in the future, but they are not ready yet.

    How accurate is the matching?

    We're constantly improving. Right now it's about 85% accurate, and we need your help to make it better.

    Can I contribute to the project?

    Absolutely! We need developers, designers, translators, and music lovers. See the section below.
