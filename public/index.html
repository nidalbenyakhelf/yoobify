    How it works

    Three simple steps to discover music across borders.

    1. Paste Your Playlists

    Drop links from Spotify or Deezer. You can paste multiple playlists to compare them and find the hidden connections between your favorite songs.

    2. We Find the Overlap

    Our algorithm identifies songs that appear in multiple playlists and analyzes their shared traits like tempo, mood, and cultural background.

    3. Discover New Music

    Based on common tracks, we suggest similar songs from other languages and cultures you might love. Expand your musical horizons effortlessly.

    Our algorithm (in simple words)

    We don't use magic. We use math and music theory.

    • Track Matching: We compare song titles, artists, and duration to find exact matches across platforms.
    • Audio Features: We analyze tempo (BPM), key, energy, and mood to find similar songs.
    • Cultural Bridges: If you like Arabic pop with 120 BPM, we'll find Turkish, Spanish, or French songs with similar vibes.

    Want to improve our algorithm? Check the contribute section below.
